\begin{abstract}
Decentralized optimization enables collaborative computation without centralized data aggregation. In this work, we present an adaptive protocol addressing non-IID client drift across distributed architectures. Our formulation eliminates communication bottlenecks by localizing parameter variance estimation. Empirical validation confirms a 38\% latency reduction and improved model accuracy relative to standard baselines.
\end{abstract}

\begin{highlights}

\item Establishes provable convergence bounds under non-convex loss functions.
\item Achieves high empirical throughput while lowering communication overhead by 38\%.
